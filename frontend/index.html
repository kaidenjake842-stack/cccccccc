Questions

Frequently asked

How do I get an asset?+

Open an asset and press the Get Asset button. Replace the placeholder link in script.js with your real download, store, or Discord ticket URL.

What does Premium mean?+

Premium assets are reserved for members who have purchased or received premium access.

Can I add my own assets?+

Yes. The entire marketplace is controlled by the assets array in script.js, so you can easily add, remove, or edit listings.

Can this connect to a real database later?+

Yes. This front end can be connected to Firebase, Supabase, a custom API, Discord OAuth, Stripe, or another backend later.
